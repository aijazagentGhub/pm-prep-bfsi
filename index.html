1. Explain "The Mom Test" in Discovery. Design
Focus on past behavior. If you ask "Would you use this?", they will lie to be nice. Ask "When was the last time you did X?"
Example: Instead of "Would you like an AI wealth manager?", ask "How did you pick your last 3 stocks?"

2. Vision vs Mission vs Strategy. Strategy
Vision is the "North Star" (Destination). Mission is the "Vehicle" (What you do). Strategy is the "Route" (How you win).
