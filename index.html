Curated resources for Strategic, Product Sense, and BFSI Domain interviews.

Focus on Vision and Strategy for leadership-level interviews.
Term Interview Definition & Application Source
Product Vision The "North Star" or long-term goal (5-10 years). It answers "What world do we want to create?".
Example: LinkedIn's vision is to create economic opportunity for every member of the global workforce.		 Ref
Product Mission The actionable "How." It explains what the company is doing now to achieve the Vision. Ref
North Star Metric (NSM) The single most important metric that captures the core value delivered to customers. In BFSI, this might be "Total Assets Under Management" or "Successful Transactions." Ref
The Mom Test Validation framework. Rule: Talk about their life, not your idea. If you ask "Would you use this?", you've failed the test. Ref
Unit Economics (LTV/CAC) Crucial for Banking PMs. How much profit does one user generate (LTV) vs. how much did it cost to acquire them (CAC)? Ref
First Principles Thinking Breaking a problem down to its basic truths and building up from there. Vital for "Design a new ATM" questions. Ref
Opportunity Solution Tree A visual aid to ensure you don't jump to solutions before understanding the "Desired Outcome" and "Opportunities." Ref
