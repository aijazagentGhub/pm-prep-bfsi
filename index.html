Explain "The Mom Test"
A methodology to validate ideas by asking about past behaviors rather than future intent. Rule: Don't ask "Would you buy this?"; ask "When was the last time you tried to solve this problem?" and "What did you do?"
Vision vs. Mission vs. Strategy?
Vision: The "Why" (5-10yr goal). Mission: The "What" (What we do daily). Strategy: The "How" (Which specific levers we pull to achieve the vision).
How to prioritize in Banking?
Use the RICE framework but apply a Regulatory Multiplier. In BFSI, compliance (KYC, AML) often overrides user delight because "Trust" is the primary product value.
What is a North Star Metric?
The single most important metric that captures the value provided to customers. Example for a Neobank: "Average number of primary transactions per user."
Explain AI Bias in Finance.
AI models can inherit historical bias (e.g., in credit scoring). PMs must ensure Model Explainability (XAI) and diverse training sets to prevent unfair loan rejections.
