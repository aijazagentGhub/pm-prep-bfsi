Complete 2026 Preparation Guide: 80+ Curated Resources

Common QuestionExpert Model Answer
1. What is the Mom Test?
Framework to avoid bias. Ask about specific past behavior, not opinions.
2. Vision vs Mission?
Vision = Why (Long term). Mission = What (Daily action).
3. How to prioritize?
RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) + Compliance weighting.
4. North Star Metric?
The single metric that best reflects the core value delivered to customers.
5. AI Bias in Finance?
Explain how to use Model Explainability to ensure fair credit lending.
6. Dealing with bad data?
Focus on data cleaning, validation pipelines, and fallback defaults.
7. Product-Led Growth?
Using the product itself as the primary driver of customer acquisition.
8. Success of a new feature?
Define a "Counter Metric" to ensure the new feature doesn't hurt existing ones.
9. Stakeholder Conflict?
Align on shared business outcomes and user data.
10. Why BFSI sector?
Focus on the impact of financial health and the complexity of regulation.
11. MVP vs MLP?
Minimum Viable (Functional) vs Minimum Lovable (Delightful).
12. Explain API to child?
A waiter in a restaurant taking orders from customers to the kitchen.
13. Customer Journey Mapping?
Visualizing every touchpoint to identify friction in the funnel.
14. Handling tech debt?
Allocate 20% of every sprint to maintenance and refactoring.
15. Price Elasticity?
How much demand changes when you change the price (crucial for loans).
16. OKRs vs KPIs?
OKRs are for change/growth; KPIs are for business as usual.
17. Network Effects?
Value increases as more users join (e.g., payment platforms).
18. Opportunity Cost?
The value of the next best thing you *didn't* build.
19. Guesstimation?
Show your math, use a MECE tree, and sanity check the final number.
20. Behavioral Story?
Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result (focus on the Result).
